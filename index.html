    About

    A cloakroom that comes to you.

    HoldMate is a smart mobile cloakroom from Coimbatore. We pick up luggage, keep it sealed in a smart locker inside a moving vehicle, and hand it back wherever you will be next, at the time you choose.

    Purpose

    To give people the freedom to travel without the burden of carrying luggage.

    Vision

    To become India's most trusted travel convenience platform — making journeys lighter, safer and more connected.

    Mission

    To build a smart network of secure luggage storage, pickup and travel convenience services, available whenever and wherever people travel.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Can't find what you need? Write to hello@holdmate.in.

    Hold + Mate. We hold your luggage, like a travel mate who carries it for you and meets you wherever you'll be next.

    You book online. A captain picks up your bags, weighs, seals and photographs them, and locks them in their own smart locker inside a vehicle. The vehicle moves on its route, and we bring your bags to the place and time you chose.

    We pick it automatically from your bag count and weight: a Bike for 1–2 small bags, an e-Auto for up to 8 bags, a Van for up to 20 bags, and a Truck for events and big groups.

    When the captain arrives, share your 4-digit OTP or show your QR code. Bags are released only when it matches, and you can check the seal number before you accept.

    Price = time + distance + weight. A base fare of ₹49, ₹9 per km, ₹6 per hour (₹3 per hour after 24 hours) and ₹3 per kg above 5 kg. Scheduled runs are 15% cheaper, Express is 50% more. These are indicative pilot rates.

    Anywhere within 15 km of Coimbatore Junction. Use "Share my location" in the Live GPS section to check if you're inside the zone.

    Yes. Bags are sealed with a numbered tag and photographed at pickup, each booking has its own locker, captains are verified, the vehicle is tracked by GPS and handover needs your OTP or QR code.

    No cash, jewellery or valuables, no perishable food, liquids that can leak, flammable or hazardous items, weapons, illegal goods or live animals.

    Yes. Cancellation is free up to 1 hour before your pickup time.

    Yes. We run luggage desks for hotels, move-in days for colleges and hostels, and parked cloakrooms for weddings and events. Use "Partner with HoldMate" to get in touch.
